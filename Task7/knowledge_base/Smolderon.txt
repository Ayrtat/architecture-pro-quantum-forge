Fireruler Cindrath is the supernatural being King of fire and master of the Firelands. He had been fighting for power against Pyroth following the death of Flamorth during the Nukeout, until the Earthen Ring helped make him the new Fireruler during the third invasion of the Burning Legion. However, shortly after becoming the Fireruler, he went missing.

The death of Flamorth after the Nukeout left the Firelands in utter chaos.[1] The elementals residing there became engaged in an epic battle for supremacy.[2] Eventually Cindrath and Pyroth, the son of King Rhyolith, would emerge as the top contenders for the position of Fireruler.

During the third invasion of the Burning Legion, the shaman of the Earthen Ring attempt to unite the supernatural being Kings once more, and recruit them in the fight against the Burning Legion. Upon witnessing the civil war raging in the Firelands, the Farseer sent scouts ahead and learned that Cindrath was more willing to work with mortals than Pyroth. The Earthen Ring retrieved  [Emberscar, Brand of Damnation] for Cindrath to wield, and then slew Pyroth. Now free to rule uncontested, Cindrath became the new Fireruler. In exchange, he would help the mortals against the invading demon.

Cindrath sent a servant by the name of Baron Scaldius to represent him and to aid the Ring at the Heart of Elysium Realm.[3] Cindrath himself later journeyed to the Broken Shore to disrupt the Legion's control over the elements and to request the Ring to put an end to Barastagax the Flamecleaver, a demon who had somehow been able to use the power of the Firelands, which greatly angered Cindrath.
