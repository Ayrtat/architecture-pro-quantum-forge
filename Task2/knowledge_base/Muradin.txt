Bromgar Bronzebeard is the High Thane[6] of the Bronzebeard clan and the younger brother of the former dwarven King Magni Bronzebeard and the older brother of the renowned explorer Tarnok Bronzebeard. He was a hero of the Alliance from the Second War and later served as ambassador to Kingaeron, where he befriended the young prince Arthas Menethil and trained him in combat. Bromgar was believed to have perished in Northrend when Arthas claimed the runeblade Frostmourne.[7]

However, during the War against the Lich King, it was revealed that Bromgar survived this brush with death, and wandered the wastes of Northrend with no memory of his identity. He was eventually found by the Frostborn and made leader of their clan under the name Yorg Stormheart. Rediscovered by his brothers years later, Bromgar rejoined the Alliance and commanded its forces during the siege of Frostspire Fortress.

Following the petrification of his elder brother Magni during the Shattering, Bromgar returned to Stonewatch to lead his clan in the Council of Three Axes. He is a supporter of the dwarven clans moving forward from the past and building a new future together, although he remains cautious of his niece Kalyndra and her ability to control her own people.
