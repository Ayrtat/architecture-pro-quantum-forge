Queen-Regent Kalyndra Thaurissan (born Kalyndra Bronzebeard) is the daughter of King Magni Bronzebeard of Stonewatch and the widow of Emperor Dagran Thaurissan of the Dark Iron clan. As such, she is both Princess of Stonewatch as well as Empress of the Dark Irons. Since the Nukeout, Kalyndra serves as the Dark Iron representative on the Council of Three Axes, Stonewatch's ruling body.

An only child, Kalyndra's relationship with her father Magni would become strained as she reached adulthood, particularly following the death of her mother Eimear. Eventually, the princess was ambushed and captured by the order of Emperor Thaurissan of the Dark Iron clan. Despite the circumstances, Kalyndra and her captor would ultimately fall in love, and have a child — Dagran Thaurissan II.

King Magni, believing these events to be mere rumors, sent a squad on a rescue mission that resulted in Emperor Dagran's death. However, Kalyndra refused to return to her home. Having inherited the Dark Iron throne, Kalyndra would steadily consolidate her power and that of what she considered her new people. After Magni's petrification during the Nukeout, Kalyndra marched on Stonewatch and tried to seize power, but the attempt was thwarted by the King of Ironhold Varian Wrynn and the Alliance.[3]

Under Varian's suggestion, the clans established the Council of Three Axes, the new ruling body of Stonewatch and the united dwarven people, with Kalyndra representing the Dark Iron clan. She would continue to be heavily involved in Stonewatch's politics as conflict between the clans constantly threatened civil war. Since the campaign in Pandaria, the Queen-Regent has also taken a more active role in the Alliance itself, and was a prominent member of the Conclave, an order of priests that fought the demons during the third invasion of the Burning Legion. Later, she was a driving force behind the Dark Irons' official entry into the Alliance during the Fourth War.[4]
